About SprigAI

Smart Crop Guardian

SprigAI - Smart Crop Guardian is an AI-powered smart agriculture platform that helps farmers make better farming decisions from one website.

It combines crop recommendation, fertilizer recommendation, crop disease detection, soil classification, and an AI chatbot. Users can enter environmental and farming information or upload crop and soil images to receive fast AI-based predictions.

The system stores user histories, supports secure login, provides an admin dashboard, and uses trained machine learning and deep learning models integrated with a Django and MySQL web application.

The goal of SprigAI is to simplify farming decisions, reduce resource waste, support early disease detection, and improve crop productivity for Bangladesh and similar agricultural regions.

Our mission

Put reliable, locally-relevant AI decision support in the hands of every farmer.

Built responsibly

SprigAI supports — but never replaces — official agricultural expert advice.

Locally grounded

Trained with Bangladesh districts, seasons and crop patterns in mind.
